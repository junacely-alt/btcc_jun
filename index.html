Our Story

Welcome to BTCC Church. We are a community of believers dedicated to the teachings of Jesus Christ. Our church serves as a sanctuary for those seeking spiritual growth, fellowship, and a deeper connection with God.

Vision

To be a light in the community, transforming lives through the power of the Gospel and raising a generation that walks in faith and integrity.

Mission

Our mission is to reach the lost, disciple the found, and send the empowered into the world to make a lasting impact for the Kingdom of God.

Youth Ministry

Empowering the next generation through worship and study.

Community Outreach

Providing food and support to our local families in need.
