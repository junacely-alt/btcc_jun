About the Church

The church was founded in 1994 as a Bible college outreach that eventually grew into a thriving congregation. It was established by Pastor Edwin A. Urieta together with his wife, Pastor Norma S. Urieta, who faithfully led the ministry in its early years. As the church continued to grow, it developed more leaders and pastors committed to serving the community and sharing God’s Word. Today, the ministry includes dedicated leaders such as Youth Pastor Raffy Fernandez and Kids Pastor Roxanne Regpala, who help nurture the spiritual growth of the next generation.

Mission

To make disciples of all nations by sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ, baptizing believers, and teaching them to obey God’s Word, nurturing them to grow in faith and live Christ-centered lives.

Vision

To be a Christ-centered church committed to raising faithful disciples, equipping leaders, and impacting communities and nations through the transforming power of the Gospel.
